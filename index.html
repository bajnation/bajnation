Who We Are

A community built for the trails and the people who love them

At Backwoods Adventure Junkies we believe in the value of community and the outdoors. Sometimes the best remedy is to just disconnect. What better way to disconnect than to gather with friends and loved ones and head out on an adventure.

Whether it’s backpacking, base camping, mountain bike riding, or overlanding, we embrace all opportunities to get out and enjoy God’s amazing creation. Overlanding is our main focus, but every outdoor activity that brings people together is welcome.

Founded in 2016, BAJ has grown into a nationwide community of adventurers who look out for each other on the trail and off.
